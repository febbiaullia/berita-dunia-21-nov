# The World Today — 21 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, for the
make.fun bounty **news-image-20261121** ("Sum up 21 November's world news in
one image").

## The stories in the picture

- **Riyadh under fire again** — Saudi defences shoot down three Houthi
  ballistic missiles aimed at the capital early on Saturday, the second
  attack on Riyadh in days, after Thursday's strike on the PIF Tower.
- **COP31 spills into Saturday** — The UN climate talks in Antalya go into
  overtime, with fossil fuels still missing from the draft deal and a
  third version of the text due on Saturday morning.
- **Malaysia to resettle 10,000 Rohingya in Türkiye** — The plan follows
  the world court's ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the
  Rohingya.
- **India fight back in Wellington** — Jaiswal and KL Rahul share a century
  opening stand as India reach 159 for 1 on day two of the first Test.
- **Also today:** Nepal's PM to visit India on 22–23 November; Indonesia
  asks Google over 19,000 removed YouTube accounts; Pakistan seal their
  first Test series win in two years; Berlin students march against
  rearmament.

## How it runs

The poster is a single 1024x1024 PNG held in this repository. The program
copies it to the output directory as `entry.png`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
